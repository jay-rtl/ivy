About Ivy Young

Ivy Young is a literary agent driven by a simple belief: the right story can change the way people see themselves, each other, and the world around them.

She is dedicated to discovering distinctive voices and unforgettable stories—works that do more than entertain, but also inspire courage, awaken imagination, challenge assumptions, and leave readers with something meaningful long after the final page.

Ivy represents authors writing science fiction, memoir, and children's literature, with a particular interest in stories that explore identity, resilience, imagination, cultural connection, personal transformation, and the human spirit.

For Ivy, representation is not simply about finding a market for a manuscript. It is about recognizing the deeper value within a story and helping an author shape that work so it can reach the readers who may need it most.

As a former secondary school teacher, Ivy brings years of experience encouraging young writers, studying literature, and helping storytellers strengthen their voices. Her background in education has given her a deep appreciation for the power of language and for the extraordinary moment when an idea becomes a story capable of connecting with another human being.

She understands that writing often begins in uncertainty.

A writer may begin with nothing more than a memory, a question, an imagined world, or a deeply personal truth. With patience, discipline, and courage, those first words can become something far greater.

That understanding shapes the way Ivy approaches every project.

She looks not only at what a manuscript is today, but at what it has the potential to become.

Born to a multicultural family with Asian and Canadian heritage, Ivy has a strong appreciation for different cultures, experiences, and ways of seeing the world. She actively seeks stories that expand perspective, invite empathy, and give readers the opportunity to experience lives and ideas beyond their own.

She believes that literature can do what few other forms of communication can:

bridge cultures, preserve memories, challenge prejudice, inspire curiosity, restore hope, and remind people that they are not alone.

At the heart of Ivy's work is a commitment to authors who have something genuine to say.

Because every meaningful story begins with someone willing to take a risk—to write the truth, imagine the impossible, or give voice to something that has never been expressed quite the same way before.

And sometimes, one courageous story is all it takes to reach thousands of people.

Every manuscript begins as one person's vision.
With the right guidance, that vision can become a story the world remembers.
